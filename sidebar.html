One last one-time step: publish your own private copy of the app.

Publish it (about 1 minute):
  1. Open Extensions → Apps Script from the menu of this spreadsheet.
  2. Top-right, click the blue Deploy button → New deployment.
  3. Click the ⚙️ gear next to "Select type" → choose Web app.
  4. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone. (The link is unguessable — only people you share it with will ever find it.)
  5. Click Deploy. If Google asks for permissions: Review → your account → Advanced → Go to … (unsafe) → Allow. That warning just means it's your own personal script, not a Google-reviewed store app.
  6. Copy the Web app URL it shows (ends in /exec) and paste it below.
